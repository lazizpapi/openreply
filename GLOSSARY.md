# OpenReply (self-hosted for one creator)

Comment-to-DM automation for one creator's Instagram account. People comment or DM a keyword and get a lead magnet. An AI assistant answers other DMs, never comments.

## People

**Owner**:
The creator who runs this instance and owns the connected Instagram account.
_Avoid_: user, admin, client

**Follower**:
Any Instagram person who comments on or DMs the owner's account, whether or not they actually follow it.
_Avoid_: user, lead, customer, commenter

**Tester**:
An Instagram account given a role on the Meta app, so Meta treats it differently from a real follower.
_Avoid_: test user

## Campaigns

**Campaign**:
A rule that turns a keyword comment, DM or story reply into a short DM flow ending with a lead magnet link.
_Avoid_: automation, flow, funnel

**Live campaign**:
A campaign that is switched on and currently answering its keywords.
_Avoid_: active campaign, running campaign

**Keyword**:
The word a follower writes to start a campaign.
_Avoid_: trigger word, code word

**Lead magnet**:
The free resource a follower receives through the link at the end of a campaign.
_Avoid_: freebie, gift, giveaway

**Opening DM**:
The first DM of a campaign, carrying a button the follower taps to continue.
_Avoid_: first message, welcome message

**Follow gate**:
A campaign step that sends the lead magnet only after the follower follows the owner's account.
_Avoid_: follow check, follow wall

**Pending button**:
A campaign button the bot sent that the follower has not tapped yet.
_Avoid_: open button, waiting button

**Nudge**:
Resending the pending button when a follower types a yes-word instead of tapping it.
_Avoid_: retry, reminder

**Yes-word**:
A short typed reply meaning "yes", such as "ha", "xa", "да", "yes" or "+".
_Avoid_: confirmation

## AI assistant

**AI assistant**:
The part that answers follower DMs and story replies that match no campaign. It never writes comments.
_Avoid_: bot, chatbot, auto-reply

**AI instructions**:
The facts the owner writes for the AI assistant. They are the only things it may tell followers.
_Avoid_: system prompt, prompt

**Bot notice**:
The line at the start of an AI reply telling the follower that an automatic assistant is answering.
_Avoid_: disclaimer, label

**Human takeover**:
The owner replying to a follower personally, after which the AI assistant stays silent in that chat for 12 hours.
_Avoid_: handoff, pause, mute

**Handover**:
The AI assistant telling a follower that the owner will reply personally, then going silent in that chat and emailing the owner.
_Avoid_: escalation, fallback

**Daily AI limit**:
The most AI replies the assistant may send in one day across all followers.
_Avoid_: quota, cap

## Meta

**Unified login**:
Meta's way of connecting an Instagram account for apps created from October 2026 onward, through Facebook Login for Business.
_Avoid_: Facebook login, business login

**Instagram login**:
The older way of connecting an Instagram account, which Meta no longer offers to new apps.
_Avoid_: Instagram business login

**Messaging window**:
The 24 hours after a follower's last message during which the account may send them DMs.
_Avoid_: reply window, 24h rule
